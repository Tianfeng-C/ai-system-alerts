Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 1, 2026

This Privacy Policy applies to Personal Infrastructure Alerts, a private SMS notification program operated by Tianfeng Chen as a sole proprietor.

Personal Infrastructure Alerts sends automated system-status and infrastructure notifications only to the authorized and opted-in mobile phone number of the program operator. No customers or third parties are enrolled in this SMS program.

Information We Collect

Personal Infrastructure Alerts may collect and store limited information necessary to provide SMS notifications, including:

  • The authorized mobile phone number used to receive SMS alerts
  • SMS opt-in and opt-out status
  • Message delivery status
  • Basic system-event information necessary to generate operational alerts

How We Use Information

Information is used only to provide private operational and infrastructure notifications, including:

  • Authentication failure alerts
  • Service outage notifications
  • Monitoring failure notifications
  • Connectivity warnings
  • System health warnings
  • Recovery notifications

Personal Infrastructure Alerts is not used for marketing, advertising, promotions, financial advice, investment recommendations, or customer communications.

Information Sharing

We do not sell or share SMS opt-in data or personal information with third parties for marketing purposes.

Mobile information and SMS consent are not shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes.

Service providers may process limited information only as necessary to deliver SMS messages and operate the notification service.

Message Frequency

Message frequency varies based on system events and incidents. There is no fixed message schedule.

Message and data rates may apply.

Opt-Out and Help

Reply STOP at any time to stop receiving SMS messages.

Reply HELP for assistance.

Program Operator

Tianfeng Chen

for private personal software automation and infrastructure monitoring purposes.
