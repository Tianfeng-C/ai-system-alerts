# Privacy Policy

---
permalink: /privacy/
---

**Last updated: October 1, 2026**

This SMS program is operated by **Luming You** as a sole proprietor for a private personal automation system.

The system sends automated infrastructure and system-status notifications only to the owner's authorized mobile phone number.

## Information We Collect

The system may collect and store limited information necessary to provide SMS alerts, including:

- The authorized mobile phone number used to receive SMS alerts
- SMS opt-in and opt-out status
- Message delivery status
- Basic system event information required to generate operational alerts

## How We Use Information

Information is used only to provide private operational notifications, including:

- Authentication failure alerts
- Service outage notifications
- Monitoring failure notifications
- Connectivity warnings
- Recovery notifications
- Other infrastructure and system-status alerts

This SMS program is not used for marketing, advertising, promotions, or customer communications.

## Information Sharing

We do not sell or share SMS opt-in data or personal information with third parties for marketing purposes.

Mobile information and SMS consent are not shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes.

Service providers may process limited information only as necessary to deliver SMS messages and operate the notification service.

## Message Frequency

Message frequency varies based on system events and incidents.

Message and data rates may apply.

## Opt-Out and Help

Reply **STOP** at any time to stop receiving SMS messages.

Reply **HELP** for assistance.

The authorized mobile number may also be removed from the private system configuration at any time.

## Contact

This SMS program is operated by:

**Luming You**

for private personal automation and infrastructure monitoring purposes.
