Privacy Policy
This SMS program is operated by Luming You as a sole proprietor for a private personal automation system.
The system sends automated infrastructure and system-status notifications only to the owner's authorized mobile phone number.
Information collected may include the mobile phone number used to receive SMS alerts, SMS opt-in status, message delivery status, and basic system event information required to generate operational alerts.
This information is used only to provide private system monitoring, infrastructure alerts, authentication failure notifications, outage notifications, and recovery notifications.
We do not sell or share your SMS opt-in data or personal information with third parties for marketing purposes.
Mobile information and SMS consent are not shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes.
Message frequency varies based on system events.
Message and data rates may apply.
The recipient may reply STOP at any time to stop receiving SMS messages or HELP for assistance.
